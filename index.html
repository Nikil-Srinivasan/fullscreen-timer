Click the hour, minute or second digits to choose which one arrow keys and the scroll wheel change — the click never starts or pauses the clock. Start and pause always go through the Start button or Space. The length can only be changed while paused; pause first if the timer is running. While it is hidden, a tap peeks at the time without changing anything.
